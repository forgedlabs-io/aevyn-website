Overview

This page explains how to delete your account for Aevyn, the mobile app operated by Forged Labs LLC. You can delete your account and the personal data associated with it at any time, using either of the two methods below. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Aevyn and go to your profile menu, then Preferences / Settings.
  2. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE when prompted.

Your account is deactivated immediately and your data is removed from active use.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion without signing in. Email us from the email address associated with your Aevyn account:

Email privacy@aevyn.io

Use the subject line “Delete my account”. We verify your identity by matching the email address of your request with the one on file, and we respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (including 45 days for California requests where applicable). Authorized agents may submit requests with written permission.

Data that is deleted

Deleting your account removes your user-scoped records, including:

  • Account information (email, username, profile details)
  • Body and health metrics (age, height, weight, sex, activity, goals, weight log, body-fat)
  • Nutrition data (meal entries and macros, barcode-scan results, food favorites, protein log)
  • Fasting data (fasting goals, sessions, and history)
  • Workout and exercise data (sessions, sets, saved workouts and plans, exercise stats)
  • Daily planner data (tasks, routines, completions, schedule preferences)
  • Journaling and self-tracking (gratitude entries, research notes, custom metrics)
  • Preferences and settings

Food photos are processed in memory for analysis and are never stored by Aevyn, so there is nothing to delete.

Data that is kept, and retention periods

After you request deletion, the following retention periods apply:

  • 30-day recovery window: your user-scoped records are kept for thirty (30) days after the request so that you may request a final export, after which they are permanently hard-deleted.
  • Backups: backup copies may persist for up to 90 days before being permanently deleted.
  • Legal and financial records: we may keep limited information longer where required by law, for tax purposes, or to resolve disputes.
  • Aggregated, de-identified data: data that can no longer identify you may be kept indefinitely for analytics and product improvement.
  • AI-usage logs: timestamp and token-count logs used for rate limiting and billing are deleted when your account is deleted.
Deleting your account does not cancel your Aevyn Pro subscription. You must cancel separately through the Apple App Store or Google Play to avoid future charges.
