1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Forged Labs LLC ("Forged Labs," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information from users of the Aevyn mobile application ("Aevyn," the "App," or the "Service").

Aevyn is a personal wellness and habit-tracking application organized around five life "pillars." It helps you plan and track your day across multiple areas — nutrition, fasting, fitness, daily routines and tasks, and lightweight journaling — together with body-metric and BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) tracking, AI-assisted scheduling and workout generation, and home-screen widgets. Aevyn is an informational and educational tool; it is not a medical device and does not provide medical, dietary, or fitness advice.

By creating an account or using Aevyn, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Aevyn is operated by Forged Labs LLC, a single-member limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Texas.

2. Age Requirements

Aevyn is intended for users who are at least 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We utilize a neutral age-screen during onboarding. Age-gate information is used only for eligibility screening and is not retained longer than necessary. No analytics, crash logs, or IP addresses are collected prior to age verification.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use Aevyn, you may provide:

  • Account information: email address, password (stored in encrypted form by our authentication provider), optional username, and optional profile details
  • Body and health metrics: age, height, weight, sex, activity level, fitness goal, optional goal weight, optional body-fat percentage; daily weight log entries
  • Nutrition data: food and meal photos you upload for AI-based macro analysis; manually entered meal names and macros; barcode scans (the barcode number, not images); food favorites; daily protein log
  • Fasting data: intermittent fasting goal, start and end timestamps, achieved duration, fasting history
  • Workout and exercise data: exercise sessions, sets, reps, weight, duration, calories burned, saved workouts and multi-week training plans; free-text prompts you submit to generate AI workouts and plans
  • Daily planner data: selected tasks, routines, custom user-created tasks, completions, postponements and skips, wake/bed times, work schedule, busy blocks, work clock-in/out times, dashboard layout
  • Journaling and self-tracking: gratitude entries (three free-text notes per day), research notes, guitar-practice logs, stretches log, user-defined custom metrics (e.g., water intake) with values and goals
  • AI prompts: free-text inputs you submit to generate workouts, training plans, and schedule analyses
  • Preferences: units, locale, time and date format, accent color, theme, workout defaults, notification settings, and AI-feature toggles

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use Aevyn, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, app version)
  • Usage data (features accessed, frequency of use, crash reports)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (not precise GPS)
  • Log data (timestamps, error reports, performance metrics)
  • Product analytics events tied to your account identifier, used to understand how features are used and to manage feature flags (see Section 7.1)

3.3 Information We Do Not Collect

Aevyn does not collect:

  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, calendar, or messages
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Government identification numbers
  • Financial account numbers (payment processing is handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat)
  • Session recordings or screen captures of your activity within the App

5. Device Permissions

Aevyn uses the following device permissions:

  • Camera — to capture food and meal photos for AI nutrition analysis, and to scan product barcodes
  • Photo Library — to let you choose existing photos from your device for nutrition analysis
  • Notifications — to send local reminders (wake-time, hydration, fasting, workouts, routines). Aevyn does not send remote push notifications and does not request a push notification token
  • Android Health Connect (Android only, read-only) — to read Active Calories Burned, Heart Rate, and Exercise Session data to refine workout calorie calculations. Only the derived calories-burned total is uploaded to your account; raw heart-rate and per-second calorie samples remain on your device
  • iOS HealthKit — Aevyn does not integrate with iOS HealthKit. We are evaluating HealthKit support for a future iOS release

You may grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's system settings. Revoking a permission may disable the corresponding feature.

6. AI Processing

Aevyn uses artificial intelligence to analyze photos and to generate workouts, training plans, and schedule recommendations. Please read this section carefully.

6.1 What Inputs Are Sent for AI Processing

The following inputs are sent to our AI subprocessor, Anthropic PBC, for processing using the Claude model family:

  • Food photos — when you upload a meal photo, the image is transmitted in compressed form (JPEG, quality reduced for efficiency) to Anthropic for macro estimation
  • Free-text workout prompts — descriptions of the workout you want to generate, plus equipment, muscle, and time filters
  • Free-text training plan inputs — your fitness goal, days per week, equipment, level, target muscles, and cardio/stretch preferences
  • Schedule analysis inputs — your wake and bed times, task and routine names, and any free-text labels you have applied to busy blocks

No account identifier, email, or stable user ID is included in the body of these AI requests. Before any food photos or related inputs are transmitted to Anthropic, we will request your explicit, in-app consent. This data is transmitted solely for processing and is not retained by Anthropic beyond 30 days, nor is it used to train Anthropic’s models. You may decline to use the AI features and continue using the non-AI features of the App.

6.2 What Happens to Your Photos

When you upload a food photo:

  • The photo is transmitted to Anthropic for AI macro analysis
  • Anthropic processes the photo and returns nutrition estimates
  • The extracted nutrition data is returned to Aevyn and saved to your meal history
  • The photo itself is not stored by Aevyn. It is processed in memory and discarded after analysis; only the structured macro data is saved

6.3 Anthropic Data Retention

Anthropic may retain API request data for up to 30 days for trust and safety purposes. Anthropic's privacy practices are described in their privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

6.4 Acceptable Use of Photo Features

You agree that you will only upload photos that:

  • You have the legal right to upload
  • Do not contain identifiable images of other people without their consent
  • Do not contain children under 13
  • Do not contain unlawful, abusive, or harmful content

You are solely responsible for the content of photos you upload.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

7.1 Service Providers (Subprocessors)

We share information with the following third-party service providers who help us operate Aevyn:

  • Supabase, Inc. — database, authentication, and file storage (stores user account data, health metrics, journal entries, food logs, and workout records)
  • Anthropic PBC — AI-based photo analysis, workout generation, training plan generation, and schedule analysis
  • Apple Inc. — iOS app distribution and in-app purchase processing (when Aevyn is available on iOS)
  • Google LLC — Android app distribution and in-app purchase processing
  • Google Health Connect — on-device read-only access to active-calorie and exercise-session data on Android. Raw health samples are read locally and are not transmitted to Forged Labs. Access to Health Connect data is tied to clear user benefit, complies with all runtime permission requirements, and you may revoke this permission at any time.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — subscription management and entitlement tracking (receives app user ID and transaction metadata only; no emails or device IDs)
  • PostHog, Inc. — product analytics, feature flags, and error tracking. Events are tied to a pseudonymous ID and may include derived fitness values or event metadata, but no raw health metrics or account IDs (such as calories, protein, fasting duration, workout type and duration). Session recordings are not enabled
  • Open Food Facts — public barcode-to-nutrition lookup. Only the barcode number is transmitted, with no user identifier (no IP address, device data, or account ID is transmitted)
  • Expo / Expo Application Services (EAS) — over-the-air app updates and build infrastructure. Standard update-check metadata only; no user content

Each provider is contractually obligated, or operates under publicly stated terms, to handle your information securely and only for the purposes we specify.

7.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Forged Labs, our users, or others.

7.3 Business Transfers

If Forged Labs is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such transfer.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service.

When you delete your account, it is immediately deactivated and your data is removed from active use. Your user-scoped records are retained for thirty (30) days so that you may request a final export, after which they are permanently hard-deleted. Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days before being permanently deleted.

Inactive accounts and associated data will be permanently deleted after 24 months of continuous inactivity. Legal hold data will be retained as required by law.

Aggregated, de-identified data may be retained indefinitely for analytics and product improvement. We will not attempt to re-identify this data except as permitted or required by law.

To enforce per-user rate limits, prevent abuse, and track AI costs, we keep AI-usage logs (timestamps and token counts) associated with your account identifier. These logs are retained for up to 12 months and are deleted when you delete your account. They are never used for advertising, profiling, or sale.

We may retain certain information longer if required by law, for tax purposes, or to resolve disputes.

You may also request a copy of your data from within the App. Data exports are written to a private storage location and expire automatically after seven (7) days.

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

We use this information solely to provide the requested services and not for cross-context behavioral advertising, profiling, or sale.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@aevyn.io. We will verify your identity by matching the email address of your request with the email address on file. Authorized agents may submit requests with written permission. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, including 45 days for California requests where applicable, and will provide a denial procedure and appeal rights where required.

9.3 European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent laws:

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

If you withdraw your consent for health data or AI processing (via in-app settings or by emailing privacy@aevyn.io), you will lose access to those specific features, but prior lawful processing remains valid.
