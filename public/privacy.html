1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Forged Labs LLC ("Forged Labs," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information from users of the Aevyn mobile application and the Aevyn companion app for Wear OS smartwatches (together, "Aevyn," the "App," or the "Service"). When we need to refer only to the smartwatch app, we call it the "Watch App."

Aevyn is a personal wellness and habit-tracking application organized around five life "pillars." It helps you plan and track your day across multiple areas: nutrition, fasting, fitness, daily routines and tasks, and lightweight journaling. It also offers body-metric and BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) tracking, AI-assisted scheduling and workout generation, home-screen widgets, and a Watch App for tracking workouts and logging food, fasts, and tasks from your wrist. Aevyn is an informational and educational tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical, dietary, or fitness advice.

Much of the information Aevyn handles is health-related. If you live in Washington, Nevada, or Connecticut, or want more detail about health data, please also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy at aevyn.io/consumer-health-data. It is also available in the App under Preferences > Privacy.

By creating an account or using Aevyn, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where the law requires your consent (for example, for health data or AI processing), we ask for it separately in the App. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Aevyn is operated by Forged Labs LLC, a single-member limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Texas.

2. Who Can Use Aevyn

Aevyn is intended only for adults 18 years of age or older. You must be at least 18 to create an account or use Aevyn, and Aevyn is not directed to anyone under 18. We ask for your date of birth once, through a neutral age screen, right after you create your account. We use your date of birth only to check eligibility. We do not store it; we keep only a record of whether you passed the screen. If the age screen shows you are under 18, the account is blocked from using Aevyn, and the App offers to delete it.

Before you pass the age screen, the App does not send analytics data. If the App crashes, it may send a crash report at any time, including before the age screen (see Section 3.3). To create your account, our authentication provider necessarily receives your email address and password, and its servers record your IP address for security purposes. We also save the username you choose.

If we learn that anyone under 18 has created an account or given us personal information, we will close the account and delete that information promptly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe someone under 18 has given us personal information, contact us at privacy@aevyn.io.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use Aevyn, you may provide:

  • Account information: email address, password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider), the username you choose (you sign in with your email address), the first name or nickname you give during setup, and optional profile details.
  • Body and health metrics: age, height, weight, sex, activity level, fitness goal, optional goal weight, optional body-fat percentage, and daily weight log entries. If you use the body-scan feature, we also collect the values read from your scan, such as weight, body-fat percentage, skeletal muscle mass, height, and age.
  • Nutrition data:
    • Food and meal photos you upload for AI-based nutrition analysis.
    • Text descriptions of food that you type in the App, or type or speak on the Watch App, for AI-based nutrition analysis.
    • Manually entered meal names and macros.
    • Barcode numbers you scan (not images).
    • Food favorites.
    • Daily protein and calorie logs.
  • Fasting data: your intermittent fasting goal, start and end times, achieved duration, and fasting history, including fasts started, ended, or edited on the Watch App.
  • Workout and exercise data:
    • Exercise sessions, sets, reps, weight, duration, and calories burned.
    • How you felt after a workout, if you choose to record it.
    • Custom exercises, saved workouts, and multi-week training plans.
    • For outdoor walks, runs, and rides tracked with the Watch App: total distance, average pace, and average speed.
  • Daily planner data: selected tasks, routines, custom user-created tasks, completions, postponements and skips, wake and bed times, work schedule, busy blocks, work clock-in/out times, and dashboard layout.
  • Journaling and self-tracking: gratitude entries, research notes, guitar-practice logs, stretches log, and user-defined custom metrics (for example, water intake) with values and goals.
  • AI inputs: the photos, text, and settings you submit to AI features (see Section 6).
  • Preferences: units, language, time and date format, theme, workout defaults, Do Not Disturb settings, notification settings, your analytics choice, and AI-feature settings.
  • Consent records: when you accepted the Terms, and whether you granted or withdrew health-data consent and AI-processing consent. Each record includes the policy version that applied.
  • Feedback and support: bug reports and feedback you submit in the App (with your app version and device platform), and emails you send us.
  • Waitlist (website only): if you join the waitlist on aevyn.io, your email address, which we use only to tell you about Aevyn's launch.

3.2 Information From Your Devices and Connected Services

  • Android Health Connect (Android, read-only, only if you grant permission): Active Calories Burned, Total Calories Burned, Heart Rate, and Exercise Session records written by other apps or devices, such as your smartwatch. We read these on your phone to estimate the calories you burned in a workout. Only the resulting calories-burned total is saved to your account. Raw heart-rate samples and per-interval calorie samples stay on your device and are not sent to our servers.
  • Watch App sensors: see Section 3.4.

3.3 Information Collected Automatically

Usage analytics (off unless you turn them on). Analytics are off by default. If you turn on "Share usage analytics" in Preferences > Privacy, and only after you pass the age screen, the App sends the following to our analytics provider:

  • Device information: such as device type and model, operating system and version, app version, screen size, language, and time zone.
  • Usage data: screens and features used, taps on buttons and controls, app open and close events, and a small number of account events (sign-up, sign-in, password reset, onboarding completed).
  • Identifiers: a random analytics ID created on your device, and your internal account ID (a random code, not your name or email).

You can turn analytics off again at any time in Preferences > Privacy.

Crash reports (always on). If the App crashes, it sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A crash report contains technical details about the error (with anything that looks like an email address removed), the app and operating system versions, and the device model and brand. It is sent under a random install ID that is not linked to your account. We designed crash reports to leave out personal information: they do not include your account ID or email address, or the health, workout, or nutrition entries you log. Crash reports are sent even when "Share usage analytics" is off.

We tell our analytics provider not to look up your location from your IP address. We do not use analytics to record your screen or sessions.

Like any internet service, our servers and service providers, including our analytics provider when it receives usage data or crash reports, receive your device's IP address when the App connects to them, and may record it in their logs. They use it to deliver the Service and to keep it secure.

3.4 Information From the Watch App

If you use the Watch App, it uses your watch's sensors through Google's Health Services for Wear OS during workouts you start:

  • Heart rate. Your live, average, and maximum heart rate for each workout are shown on your watch and sent to the App on your paired phone. Heart-rate data is processed only on your watch and phone. It is never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party.
  • Activity and calories. The watch estimates calories burned and activity during a workout. The workout's calories, duration, type, and logged sets are sent to the App and saved to your account like any other workout.
  • Distance, pace, and speed (outdoor workouts only). For outdoor walks, runs, and rides, and only if you allow location access, your watch uses GPS to measure total distance, average pace, and average speed. These totals are saved to your account with the workout. Your coordinates and route are processed only inside your watch's Health Services to calculate distance. The Watch App never reads them, and they are not stored, sent to your phone, or sent to us.
  • Food, fasting, and tasks. Food descriptions, fasts, and task completions you enter on the watch are sent to the App and handled exactly as if you had entered them on your phone. Food descriptions are processed by AI only if you have enabled AI features and given AI consent (Section 6).
  • Voice entry. If you speak a food description on the watch, your watch's built-in speech-recognition service turns your speech into text. That service is provided by your watch's operating system (for example, Google) under its own terms. Aevyn receives only the resulting text, never audio.
  • Diagnostics. The Watch App keeps a short event log on the watch (for example, "workout started" or "GPS unavailable") to help troubleshoot problems. It contains no health readings or locations, and it is not sent to us.

How the watch and phone communicate. The Watch App sends data to the App on your phone through Google Play services' Wearable Data Layer. When your devices are connected by Bluetooth, data travels directly over an encrypted Bluetooth link. When they are not, Google Play services may relay the data through Google's servers. Google states that this relayed data is end-to-end encrypted and readable only by the Aevyn app. The Watch App does not connect to the internet or to our servers on its own. Anything that reaches our servers is sent by the App on your phone, under your account.

You can use the Watch App without the phone App and without an account. In that case, your workout history stays on the watch until a paired phone with the App connects.

3.5 Information We Do Not Collect

Aevyn does not collect:

  • Your location coordinates or location history. The Watch App turns on GPS only during outdoor workouts you start, and only to calculate distance, pace, and speed on the watch (Section 3.4). We never receive coordinates or routes. The phone App does not request location access.
  • Audio recordings. We never receive audio. Watch voice entry is converted to text on your watch.
  • Contacts, calendar, or messages.
  • Government identification numbers.
  • Payment card or bank account numbers. Payments are handled by Google Play and Apple, and subscription status is handled by RevenueCat.
  • Screen or session recordings of your activity within the App.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate Aevyn across nutrition, fasting, fitness, daily planning, and journaling features.
  • Estimate nutrition from food photos and food descriptions, and read values from body-scan images, using artificial intelligence (Section 6).
  • Generate AI-assisted workouts, workout suggestions, multi-week training plans, and schedule recommendations based on your inputs.
  • Calculate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and related metrics from your age, height, weight, and sex, and keep a running calorie balance from your food and workout activity.
  • Estimate calories burned during workouts from Health Connect and Watch App data.
  • Show live workout information (such as heart rate, calories, distance, and pace) on your watch and phone, and keep the Watch App and the App in sync.
  • Maintain your meal, fasting, and workout history, plus your routines and personal dashboard, and restore them if you reinstall the App or use a new device.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb during workouts, if you enable that feature.
  • Send local reminders and notifications you have turned on.
  • Process subscription purchases and manage your subscription status.
  • Send account emails, such as password-reset codes.
  • Respond to your feedback, support requests, and privacy requests.
  • Find and fix crashes, and, if you turn on usage analytics, understand how the App is used and improve features (Section 3.3).
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

What we do not do:

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use health information from Health Connect or the Watch App for advertising.
  • We do not use your personal information to train AI models, and our AI provider does not use it to train its models (Section 6).

5. Device Permissions

Aevyn asks for the following permissions. Each one is used only for the feature described.

Phone App:

  • Camera: to take food and meal photos and body-scan photos for AI analysis, and to scan product barcodes.
  • Photos: to let you choose existing photos for nutrition or body-scan analysis.
  • Notifications and alarms: to send local reminders (wake-time, hydration, fasting, workouts, routines) at the times you choose. Aevyn does not send remote push notifications.
  • Do Not Disturb access (Android, optional): to turn Do Not Disturb on during a workout and restore your previous setting afterwards, only if you enable this feature.
  • Android Health Connect (Android, read-only, optional): to read Active Calories Burned, Total Calories Burned, Heart Rate, and Exercise Session data to refine workout calorie estimates. See Section 3.2 for what leaves your device.
  • iOS HealthKit: Aevyn does not currently integrate with HealthKit.

Watch App (Wear OS):

  • Body sensors / Heart rate: to measure your heart rate during workouts you start. On Wear OS 6 and later, this permission appears as "Heart rate."
  • Physical activity: to estimate calories and activity during workouts.
  • Location (precise and approximate): requested the first time you start an outdoor walk, run, or ride. It is used only while that workout runs, to calculate distance, pace, and speed, and an ongoing workout notification is shown the whole time. The Watch App never requests background location access. You can choose "No GPS" to track a workout without distance.
  • Notifications: to show the ongoing workout notification, and to let you know when a workout is running on your phone.
  • Vibration and keeping the device awake: to buzz when a rest timer ends, even with the screen off.

If you deny the sensor permissions, the Watch App still works as a workout timer. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's system settings (on a Wear OS watch: Settings > Apps > Aevyn > Permissions). Revoking a permission may disable the related feature, but the rest of the App keeps working.

6. AI Processing

Aevyn uses artificial intelligence to analyze photos and text and to generate workouts, training plans, and schedule recommendations. AI features are optional. They run only if you have given AI-processing consent and the AI setting is on. We check your consent on our servers before any request is sent to our AI provider. You can turn AI off at any time in Preferences > AI, and you can keep using every non-AI feature.

6.1 What Is Sent for AI Processing

When you use an AI feature, the following inputs are sent through our servers to our AI service provider, Anthropic PBC, which processes them using its Claude models:

  • Food photos: the image, compressed, for a nutrition estimate.
  • Food descriptions: text you type in the App, or type or speak on the Watch App, describing what you ate.
  • Body-scan photos: a photo of a body-composition printout or smart-scale screen, so the values can be read and filled in for you. Printouts sometimes show other details, such as a name or member ID. Please crop or cover anything you don't want sent.
  • Workout generation and suggestions: your free-text request, chosen filters (equipment, muscles, time), the available exercise list, and, for workout suggestions, a summary of your recent training (such as days since you last did an exercise, your last weight used, and muscle-recovery estimates).
  • Training plan inputs: your fitness goal, days per week, equipment, level, target muscles, cardio and stretch preferences, and any notes you add.
  • Schedule analysis inputs: your wake and bed times, task and routine names, and any labels you have given busy blocks.

We do not include your name, email address, username, or account ID in these requests. Our servers keep a record of each AI request's date, feature type, size (token count), and estimated cost, linked to your account, for rate limiting and cost tracking. This record does not include the content of your request. If we have set a different daily AI limit for your account (for example, for a tester), we also keep that limit, its end date, and a short note with your account. These records are included in your data export (see Sections 8 and 9).

6.2 What Happens to Your Photos and Text

  • The photo or text is sent to Anthropic for analysis.
  • Anthropic returns structured results, such as estimated calories and macros, or the values read from a body scan.
  • Aevyn saves only the structured results (for example, a meal entry or your body metrics).
  • The photo or description itself is not stored by Aevyn. It is processed in memory and discarded after the analysis.

6.3 AI Provider Retention

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic does not use our API inputs or outputs to train its models. Anthropic generally deletes them within 30 days. If a request is flagged under Anthropic's Usage Policy, Anthropic may keep the inputs and outputs for up to 2 years and related safety scores for up to 7 years, and it may keep data longer where the law requires. See Anthropic's privacy information at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy and https://privacy.claude.com.

AI estimates can be wrong. Please review AI-generated nutrition values, body-scan readings, and workouts before relying on them.

6.4 Acceptable Use of Photo Features

You agree that you will only upload photos that:

  • you have the legal right to upload;
  • do not contain identifiable images of other people without their consent;
  • do not contain minors (anyone under 18); and
  • do not contain unlawful, abusive, or harmful content.

You are solely responsible for the content of photos you upload.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service, and then as described below.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account at any time in the App (profile menu > Preferences > Account > Delete account), or by emailing privacy@aevyn.io from the email address on your account. Our web page at aevyn.io/delete-account explains both options. When you request deletion:

  • Your account is deactivated immediately, and you can no longer sign in.
  • We keep your account data for 30 days so you can still request a final copy of your data by emailing privacy@aevyn.io. After 30 days, an automated nightly process permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it. That includes your profile, health and body metrics, meal, fasting, and workout history, tasks and routines, journal entries, consent records, AI-usage records, and any AI limit setting.
  • We delete the analytics data linked to your account from our analytics provider within 60 days of your request.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store to avoid future charges.

Backups. Deleted data may remain in our database provider's backups until those backups expire, which happens within 30 days. We do not restore deleted accounts from backups.

Feedback. If you sent us feedback or a bug report, we may keep the message after your account is deleted, without your account ID attached, to keep improving the App. Emails you send us stay in our email system for as long as we need them to handle your request and for our records.

Specific retention periods:

  • AI-usage and rate-limit records (date, feature type, token count, and estimated cost, linked to your account): automatically deleted after 12 months, or sooner if you delete your account.
  • AI limit settings (only if we have set one for your account): kept while your account exists. They stop applying after their end date.
  • Data exports: when you request an export, the App gets the file straight from our servers and saves it on your device. We do not keep a copy on our servers. Once it is on your device, the file is yours to store, share, or delete.
  • Consent records: kept while your account exists, as proof of your choices.
  • Waitlist emails: kept until Aevyn launches and we have notified you, or until you ask us to remove you.

Data stored only on your devices. Some data exists only on your phone or watch, such as watch workout history (including heart rate), the watch's diagnostic log, and some local App settings. It is not on our servers, so deleting your account does not remove it. To remove it, uninstall the App and the Watch App, or clear their data in your device settings. If you have turned on device backup, your phone or watch may include App data, including watch workout history with heart rate, in your own Google or Apple account backup, which is governed by their terms.

De-identified data. We may keep aggregated or de-identified data that can no longer reasonably identify you. We will not try to re-identify it except as the law permits.

Legal holds. We may keep specific information longer if the law requires it, or to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements. If we do, we will keep only what is needed.

9. Your Privacy Rights

9.1 All Users

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Access and correct your information in the App.
  • Get a copy of your data. Use the in-App export (Preferences > Account > Export my data), or email privacy@aevyn.io.
  • Delete individual entries (meals, fasts, workouts) in the App, or your entire account (Section 8).
  • Withdraw consent:
    • Turn off AI features in Preferences > AI.
    • Turn off usage analytics in Preferences > Privacy.
    • Revoke Health Connect, sensor, or location permissions in your device settings.
    • To withdraw consent to the processing of your health data, delete your account or email privacy@aevyn.io. Because health data is central to Aevyn, the App cannot work without it.
  • Control the Watch App: turn off heart-rate, activity, or location access in your watch settings; choose "No GPS" or "Timer only" when starting a workout; or uninstall the Watch App.
  • Appeal any decision we make about your request (Section 9.4).

9.2 Consumer Health Data (Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, and Other States)

The information described in Sections 3.1 through 3.4, such as body metrics, nutrition, fasting, workouts, calories, and heart rate, is "consumer health data" under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law, and Connecticut's data privacy law. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (aevyn.io/consumer-health-data) explains:

  • the categories of consumer health data we collect and why;
  • where it comes from;
  • who we share it with; and
  • how you can confirm, access, delete, and withdraw consent for that data, and appeal our decisions.

That policy applies in addition to this one.

9.3 Residents of Other U.S. States With Privacy Laws

Depending on where you live (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas, Virginia, and other states with consumer privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data, and access it;
  • correct inaccurate personal data;
  • delete personal data;
  • get a portable copy of your personal data;
  • get a list of the third parties we have disclosed your personal data to;
  • opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising, and profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects. We do none of these.
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information. We use sensitive personal information, including health data, only to provide the Service you request, with your consent where required.
  • be free from discrimination for exercising these rights.

We honor these requests for all U.S. users, whether or not a particular state's law applies to us.

California. We do not currently meet the California Consumer Privacy Act's thresholds for a covered "business," but we offer the rights above to California residents voluntarily. We collect the categories of personal information described in Section 3, including sensitive personal information (health and fitness data), for the purposes in Section 4. We disclose them only to the service providers in Section 7.1. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. Retention periods are in Section 8.

9.4 How to Make a Request, and How to Appeal

To make a request, email privacy@aevyn.io from the email address on your account, or use the in-App tools described above. We verify your identity by matching your request to the email address on file. If you email from a different address, we may ask you to confirm from the account address.

An authorized agent may make a request for you with your written permission. We may need to verify your identity directly.

We respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend that by another 45 days where the law allows, and we will tell you why. We do not charge for requests unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Appeals. If we deny your request in whole or in part, you may appeal by replying to our decision, or by emailing privacy@aevyn.io with "Privacy Appeal" in the subject line. We will respond in writing within 45 days (or a shorter period if your state requires it) and explain our decision. If we deny your appeal, you may contact your state Attorney General, for example:

  • Washington: https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint
  • Connecticut: https://portal.ct.gov/AG/Common/Complaint-Form-Landing-page
  • Texas: https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint

9.5 "Do Not Track" and Opt-Out Signals

We do not track you across other websites or apps, and we do not allow third parties to do so through Aevyn. Because we do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, we have nothing to stop in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals or Global Privacy Control. If we change that, we will honor those signals as required by law and update this policy first.

Aevyn is intended only for users in the United States. We offer it only in the United States, and it is not directed to people in any other country. If you use Aevyn while traveling outside the United States, your information is still handled as described in Section 11, under U.S. law, where privacy laws may differ from those of the country you are in.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS.
  • Encryption of stored data by our database provider.
  • Hashed passwords, handled by our authentication provider.
  • Row-level security, so each account can access only its own records.
  • Server-side checks that you are signed in and have given consent before any AI request is made.
  • Restricted, need-to-know access to personal data, limited to the founder and any contractor bound by confidentiality.
  • Encrypted watch-to-phone transfers (Section 3.4).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you, and regulators where required, as the law requires. That includes the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Health Breach Notification Rule and state breach-notification laws.
