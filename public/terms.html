4. Description of the Service

Aevyn is a personal wellness and habit-tracking application organized around five life "pillars." It helps you plan and track your day across multiple areas, including:

  • Daily planner — a task and routine planner with optional AI-assisted scheduling and schedule analysis
  • Nutrition logging — meal photo capture (analyzed by AI for macro estimation), manual meal entry, barcode scanning, calorie and macro tracking, and an all-time calorie-balance ledger
  • Intermittent fasting — fasting timer with stage tracking and history
  • Workout and exercise tracking — exercise session logging (sets, reps, weight, duration, calories), AI-generated workouts and multi-week training plans, muscle-recovery insights, and optional Android Health Connect activity import
  • Body metrics and BMR — age, height, weight, body fat, daily weight log, and BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) and TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) calculation
  • Lightweight journaling and self-tracking — gratitude entries, research notes, guitar-practice logs, stretches, and user-defined custom metrics
  • Dashboard and home-screen widgets — a customizable dashboard and Android home-screen widgets that mirror task, calorie, fasting, nutrition, and workout data

Aevyn is an informational and educational tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical, dietary, or fitness advice. Aevyn may be updated, modified, or discontinued at our discretion.
