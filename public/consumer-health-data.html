This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy applies to "consumer health data" that Forged Labs LLC ("Forged Labs," "we," "us") collects through the Aevyn mobile app, the Aevyn Wear OS watch app, and aevyn.io (together, "Aevyn"). It is written to meet Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373), Nevada's consumer health data law (NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550), and Connecticut's consumer health data provisions. We apply it to all users, wherever they live. It applies in addition to the Aevyn Privacy Policy at aevyn.io/privacy (also in the App under Preferences > Privacy).

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status. That includes information derived or inferred from other data.

1. Consumer Health Data We Collect, and Why

Body measurements

Examples: age, sex, height, weight, daily weight log, body-fat percentage, goal weight, activity level, fitness goal, and values read from a body-scan image (such as skeletal muscle mass). How we use it: to calculate your BMR, calorie targets, and progress, and to show your history and dashboard.

Nutrition and eating patterns

Examples: meals, foods, calories and macros, protein log, food favorites, and food photos and food descriptions you submit for AI analysis (processed and then discarded). How we use it: to track nutrition, estimate nutrition from photos and descriptions, and show your calorie balance.

Fasting

Examples: fasting goal, start and end times, durations, and history. How we use it: for the fasting timer, history, and reminders.

Exercise and activity

Examples: workouts, sets, reps, weights, duration, calories burned, how you felt after a workout, training plans, and muscle-recovery estimates; for outdoor watch workouts, total distance, average pace, and average speed. How we use it: to track workouts, suggest and generate workouts and plans, and estimate recovery.

Vital signs and sensor data

Examples: heart rate from the Aevyn watch app or from Android Health Connect, and calories-burned records from Health Connect. How we use it: to show live heart rate and estimate calories burned. Heart-rate data is processed only on your devices and is never uploaded to our servers.

Health-related inferences

Examples: calorie balance, BMR, recovery percentages, and AI-generated suggestions based on the data above. How we use it: to personalize the App's features and recommendations.

Health-related app activity

Examples: which health features you use, such as starting a fast or logging a meal, recorded in our usage analytics. How we use it: to fix bugs and improve features. We collect this only if you turn on "Share usage analytics" in Preferences > Privacy. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

Health consent records

Examples: whether and when you gave or withdrew health-data and AI consent. How we use it: as proof of your choices, and to honor a withdrawal.

We collect consumer health data to provide the features you ask for, and for the other purposes above, with your consent where the law requires it. We do not use it for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not use it to train AI models.

2. Sources of Consumer Health Data

We collect consumer health data from:

  • You: what you enter, photograph, or say (watch voice entry is turned into text on your watch).
  • Your devices: the Aevyn watch app's sensors (heart rate, activity, and GPS-based distance during outdoor workouts, as described in our Privacy Policy) and Android Health Connect, if you grant permission.
  • Our own calculations and AI analysis: the inferences and estimates described above.

3. Consumer Health Data We Share

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

We disclose consumer health data only to service providers (processors). They process it on our behalf, under agreements that limit their use to providing services to us:

  • Cloud database and hosting: Supabase, Inc. All categories stored in your account (not heart rate).
  • AI service provider: Anthropic PBC. Food photos, food descriptions, body-scan photos, workout and plan inputs, and training summaries you submit to AI features (only with your AI consent).
  • Product analytics: PostHog, Inc. Health-related app activity (which features you use), tied to a random ID and your internal account ID, never your email, and only if "Share usage analytics" is on. No workout, nutrition, body, or heart-rate values.
  • Data transfer between your own devices: Google LLC (Google Play services' Wearable Data Layer). Workout data (including heart rate), food, fasting, and task entries moving between your watch and your phone. The data is end-to-end encrypted when relayed through Google's servers.

We may also disclose consumer health data when the law requires it (for example, a valid court order), and in a merger or acquisition, where we will ask for your consent when the law requires it.

We do not have affiliates that receive consumer health data.

We do not use geofences around health care facilities, and we do not collect location data that shows whether you are seeking health care.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm and access. Confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data; get a copy of it; and get a list of all third parties and affiliates we have shared it with, with contact information for each.
  • Delete. Have your consumer health data deleted. That includes deletion from our backups, and notice of your request to our processors. Backups expire on their own within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent. Withdraw your consent to our collection or sharing of your consumer health data. Withdrawal stops future collection and sharing. Because health data is central to Aevyn, withdrawing consent to all health-data processing means closing your account. You can withdraw specific consents without closing your account:
    • AI processing: Preferences > AI.
    • Usage analytics: Preferences > Privacy.
    • Health Connect, heart-rate, and location access: your device's permission settings.
  • Non-discrimination. We will not treat you differently because you exercise these rights.

How to exercise your rights

  • In the App: export your data in Preferences > Account > Export my data; delete your account in Preferences > Account > Delete account; turn off AI and usage analytics in Preferences.
  • On the web: aevyn.io/delete-account.
  • By email: privacy@aevyn.io, from the email address on your account. Please say which right you are exercising.

We verify requests by matching them to your account's email address. We respond within 45 days. Where the law allows, we may extend that once by another 45 days, and we will tell you why. Requests are free up to twice a year.

Deletion timing

When you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately. We keep your data for 30 days so you can request a final export, and then delete it permanently. That is well within the 45-day response period.

Appeals

If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, or by emailing privacy@aevyn.io with "Consumer Health Data Appeal" in the subject line. We will respond in writing within 45 days and explain our decision. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General:

  • Washington: https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint
  • Nevada: https://ag.nv.gov/Complaints/CSU_Complaints___FAQ/
  • Connecticut: https://portal.ct.gov/AG/Common/Complaint-Form-Landing-page
